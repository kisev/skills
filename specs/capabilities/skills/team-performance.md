# `team-performance`

## Purpose

Turn recorded journal history into a quarterly performance summary, bonus or
raise justification, or growth-plan draft for one report.

## Triggers and Near-Misses

Trigger for periodic performance artifacts; near-miss: a single difficult
conversation or a team retrospective.

## Inputs and Outputs

Input is the person, a strict period, journal facts, profile levels, optional
delivery evidence, and target-format constraints. Output is a workspace
artifact plus a journal completion note.

## Workflow Stages

Establish scope, load evidence, build the fact base, draft the document,
record and hand over, verify thin-evidence warnings.

## Requirements

### REQ-F-536 - Ground performance claims

Every claim shall link to a journal entry date; unlinked characteristics shall
be marked assumptions or dropped. Former identifier: `REQ-TEAM-PERF-01`,
normalized without a behavior change on 2026-09-28.

#### Verification

An assessment built from thin evidence cites dated entries and labels unsupported claims.

### REQ-F-537 - Keep performance publication manual

The skill shall never submit to external HR systems. Former identifier:
`REQ-TEAM-PERF-02`, normalized without a behavior change on 2026-09-28.

#### Verification

Preparation returns the assessment without invoking a remote submission operation.
