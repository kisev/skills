# `team-1on1`

## Purpose

Prepare one 1:1 with one named report from open commitments and recorded
history, then record its outcomes into the journal.

## Triggers and Near-Misses

Trigger for 1:1 preparation or recording 1:1 outcomes; near-miss: summarizing a
supplied transcript without the people context.

## Inputs and Outputs

Input is the person, the people profile, journal history, and optional
messaging threads from exact URLs. Output is a bounded preparation note and,
after the meeting, dated journal entries.

## Workflow Stages

Resolve the person and cadence, load journal context, build an agenda ordered
by proven commitments, draft the preparation, record outcomes, and verify.

## Requirements

- REQ-TEAM-1ON1-01: agenda items trace to journal entries or profile facts;
  fabricated history is out of contract.
- REQ-TEAM-1ON1-02: outcomes are recorded as bounded facts, not transcripts.
