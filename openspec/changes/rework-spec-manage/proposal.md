# Proposal

## Why

spec-manage — strong rework decided in the foundation renegotiation. This change gives the component its exhaustive
specification and executes the renegotiation decisions that concern it.

## What Changes

Today the owner of the legacy specs/ tree: spec-init/onboard/update/review modes, 19 mandatory READMEs, arc42 templates in two languages, REQ-\*/ADR lifecycle, and a validator.

Decided: the in-house spec system is retired in favor of OpenSpec; this skill becomes the OpenSpec companion family — maintaining the generalization layer (charter, capability map, cross-cutting decisions) and onboarding projects into OpenSpec, complementing OpenSpec's own workflows.
Facts: the heaviest skill (448KB), 75% of it bilingual normative templates whose fate is decided here.
Open: companion family design, template migration or retirement, exhaustive capability spec.

None yet — the exhaustive capability spec for this component is drafted
inside this change when it is picked up.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

`skills/spec-manage`, the legacy `specs/` tree, and the new companion layer.
