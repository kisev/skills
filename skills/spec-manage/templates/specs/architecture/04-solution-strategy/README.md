# 04 Solution Strategy

## Purpose

Summarize the few fundamental design choices that shape the target architecture and explain why they satisfy its critical goals.

## Included

- Top-level decomposition approach, key technology choices, architectural patterns, dependency direction, and strategies for critical quality goals.
- Direct links to the requirements and ADRs that justify each material strategy.

## Excluded

- Detailed building blocks, runtime scenarios, technology inventories, implementation plans, and decisions too local or trivial for architecture.

## Decomposition Rules

Keep the strategy summary in this `README.md`; place detailed decisions in ADRs and detailed mechanisms in their owning viewpoints. Do not create child files.

## Expected Structure

Organize around a small set of material strategies, each with rationale and direct requirement or ADR links.

## Content Template

Replace this guidance with the target decomposition, core patterns and technologies, dependency strategy, and means of meeting architecture-driving requirements.
