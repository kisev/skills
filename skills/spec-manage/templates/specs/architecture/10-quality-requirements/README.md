# 10 Quality Requirements

## Purpose

Explain how the architecture provides the measurable or verifiable qualities owned by `REQ-Q-*`.

## Included

- Direct links to architecture-driving `REQ-Q-*` entries and the mechanisms, trade-offs, and verification hooks that provide them.
- Architectural responses to measurable security, reliability, performance, usability, and maintainability properties.

## Excluded

- Repeated normative thresholds, aspirational wishes, mechanism details owned by another viewpoint, test plans, and improvement backlogs.

## Decomposition Rules

Keep the quality view in this `README.md`; do not create child files. Organize by linked `REQ-Q-*` owner or material quality scenario.

## Expected Structure

For each architecture-driving quality requirement, state the providing mechanisms and relevant trade-offs, then link to their owning viewpoints instead of duplicating details.

## Content Template

Replace this guidance with linked `REQ-Q-*` entries, their target-state architecture mechanisms, interactions among qualities, and observable verification hooks.
